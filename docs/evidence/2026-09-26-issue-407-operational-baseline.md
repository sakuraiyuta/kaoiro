## Freeze procedure (before the first server switch)

1. Take a new snapshot of every sidecar, transcript and conversation store
   modified since the start of the baseline window. Record the manifest and
   archive hashes here.
2. Freeze the cohort before looking at any outcome:
   - the candidate table with every role filled in from the sidecars
   - the data-availability matrix
   - the selection rule stated above
3. After the switch, record the actual `T_server`. Select the latest five
   eligible completions mechanically. For each one, record:
   - the assignment and SDK session IDs
   - the window timestamps
   - the engine/backend/model mix
   - the task category
   - the deliverable and test scope
   - the completion criteria
4. If fewer than five are eligible, or required records could not be preserved,
   mark the comparison incomplete here and report it before rollout.
